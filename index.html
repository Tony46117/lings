FAQ

Common questions

How long before my trees start fruiting?

Grafted passion fruit seedlings fruit in 6–9 months, apples in 2–3 years, and avocados in 3–4 years depending on variety and care.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes — we deliver nationwide via trusted couriers. Delivery is free for bulk orders of 50+ seedlings within Nairobi.

Are the seedlings really grafted?

Yes. All apple and avocado seedlings are grafted onto disease-free rootstock for true-to-type fruit and stronger trees.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, bank transfer or cash on delivery (Nairobi). We confirm details on WhatsApp when you place your order.
